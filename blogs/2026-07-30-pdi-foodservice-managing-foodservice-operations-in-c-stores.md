What Is PDI Foodservice? PDI Foodservice gives convenience retailers one system for running a foodservice program, from inventory to staffing to compliance. It tracks ingredients from vendor delivery through to the warmer, ties labor scheduling to food service sales data, and keeps nutritional and allergen information current in the pricebook as ingredients change.
