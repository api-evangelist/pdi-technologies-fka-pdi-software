What Are PDI Electronic Shelf Labels? PDI Electronic Shelf Labels (ESL) replace paper price tags with a digital system connected directly to a retailer’s ERP pricebook. When a price changes in the pricebook, every shelf label updates automatically across the store network: no printing and no staff pulled off the floor to swap tags.
