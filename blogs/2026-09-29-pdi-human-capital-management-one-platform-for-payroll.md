What Is PDI Human Capital Management? PDI Human Capital Management (HCM) is a cloud-based workforce platform built in partnership with Paycor and connected directly to PDI Enterprise. It brings payroll, time tracking, labor scheduling, and talent management into one system, so retailers can manage their workforce without switching between tools.
